For this project, I focused on power electronics and board-level hardware design by engineering a custom buck converter.

### Hardware Design & Fabrication
* **Power Management:** Architected a step-down DC-to-DC converter circuit to efficiently regulate voltage for downstream electronic components.
* **PCB Layout:** Routed and optimized the printed circuit board layout to minimize noise and handle thermal dissipation effectively.
* **Fabrication & Assembly:** Managed the hardware prototyping lifecycle, from schematic capture to component sourcing through DigiKey and final board manufacturing through JLCPCB.

**Technologies Used:** PCB Design, Power Electronics, Voltage Regulation, Hardware Prototyping.
