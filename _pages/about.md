### What I do

I am an electrical and computer engineering student dedicated to hardware design and translating complex logic into optimized physical architectures. My primary focus is on hardware specification and RTL design. I spend my time working deeply with Verilog to model circuit behavior, synthesize custom digital logic, and understand how systems operate at the foundational level.

While my core passion lies in building and verifying physical hardware, I also write low-level C and C++ code to effectively interface with and test the systems I design. Having a firm grasp on strict memory management and data structures allows me to better understand hardware-software integration and thoroughly validate my logic designs. My ultimate goal is to contribute to next-generation microchip development through hands-on experience.
